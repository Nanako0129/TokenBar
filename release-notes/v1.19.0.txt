Before you update:
- Tabs for providers you never set up disappear. v1.18.0 began including configured quota sources in the tab bar alongside local usage, which is right for a provider you use and wrong for one you don't. Codex, Claude and Antigravity are built as cards that always exist, so the presence of a card said nothing about whether anything had been set up, and Codex and Antigravity took a tab on every install. A provider with no credential at all now has no tab. One whose credential exists and cannot be read - a permission problem, a corrupt file - keeps its tab and shows what went wrong.
- Antigravity's two tabs become one. Its IDE and CLI clients draw on one subscription and had a tab each. They now share one, labelled Antigravity, the arrangement Grok Build and Grok Bot already use: the CLI's usage and the IDE's quota card sit under it together. Your own settings survive the merge - a hidden Antigravity CLI tab stays hidden, and its place in your saved tab order becomes the merged tab's.

Fixes:
- An agy installed after TokenBar started was never found. Antigravity's CLI discovery cached its answer for the life of the process, including the answer that there was nothing to find. Installing agy while TokenBar was running therefore changed nothing until the next launch, and the card went on reporting the error it had been given before. An empty result is now retried on a later refresh.
